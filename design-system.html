Principles

01
間
Ma — negative space

Emptiness is a material. Let generous margins and quiet gaps carry as much weight as the content. Never fill a slide edge to edge.

寂
Restraint

One accent, one idea per surface. Sumi-ink type on warm paper; the persimmon seal appears once, never as decoration. Subtract before you add.

侘
Imperfect calm

Soft hairlines, a faint paper grain, an unclosed ensō circle. Nothing crisp or synthetic — warmth and a hand-made hush throughout.
